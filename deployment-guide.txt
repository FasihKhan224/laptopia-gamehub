Laptopia & GameHub - Deployment Guide
========================================

Congratulations on getting your website built! It is currently deployed and live at:
https://FasihKhan224.github.io/laptopia-gamehub/

This guide will show you how to buy a custom domain (like laptopia.com) and connect it to your site, as well as how to switch to a real database when you're ready.

---
STEP 1: BUYING A CUSTOM DOMAIN
---

1. Go to a domain registrar like Namecheap (https://www.namecheap.com) or GoDaddy (https://www.godaddy.com).
2. Search for your desired name, for example: `laptopia.pk` or `laptopiagamehub.com`.
3. Add it to your cart and complete the purchase.

---
STEP 2: CONNECTING YOUR DOMAIN TO GITHUB PAGES
---

Once you own the domain, you need to point it to your GitHub Pages site.

1. Configure DNS on your Domain Registrar:
   - Log into your Namecheap/GoDaddy account and find "DNS Settings" or "Manage DNS" for your domain.
   - Add four "A Records" pointing to GitHub's IP addresses:
     - 185.199.108.153
     - 185.199.109.153
     - 185.199.110.153
     - 185.199.111.153
   - Add a "CNAME Record" with host "www" pointing to `FasihKhan224.github.io`.

2. Add the Domain to GitHub:
   - Go to your GitHub repository: https://github.com/FasihKhan224/laptopia-gamehub
   - Click "Settings" (the gear icon at the top).
   - On the left sidebar, click "Pages".
   - Scroll down to the "Custom domain" section.
   - Type in your new domain (e.g., `laptopia.pk`) and click "Save".
   - GitHub will check the DNS. Once verified, check the "Enforce HTTPS" box to secure your site.

It may take up to 24 hours for the DNS changes to propagate globally, but usually it works within an hour.

---
STEP 3: CONNECTING THE DATABASE (SUPABASE)
---

Right now, your admin panel and orders are saved in the browser (localStorage) for demonstration purposes. This means only you can see the orders you make on your computer. 

To make it fully workable for real customers, connect it to a free Supabase database:

1. Create a Database:
   - Go to https://supabase.com and create a free account.
   - Click "New Project" and give it a name (e.g., LaptopiaDB).
   - Create a strong database password and click "Create new project". Wait for it to provision.

2. Setup the Tables:
   - In Supabase, go to the "SQL Editor" on the left menu.
   - Open the file `supabase/schema.sql` from your project folder.
   - Copy all the text inside `schema.sql`, paste it into the Supabase SQL Editor, and click "Run".
   - This creates all the necessary tables (products, orders, inquiries).

3. Connect Your Code:
   - In Supabase, go to "Project Settings" (gear icon) > "API".
   - Copy your "Project URL" and "anon public" API Key.
   - Open `js/supabase-config.js` in your code editor.
   - Paste the URL and Key into lines 8 and 9 where it says `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`.

4. Deploy Database Changes:
   - Once you save `js/supabase-config.js`, you need to push the changes to GitHub.
   - Open your terminal and run:
     git add .
     git commit -m "Connected Supabase database"
     git push
   - GitHub Actions will automatically deploy the changes to your live site!

Now your admin panel will securely store orders in the cloud!
